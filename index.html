Your Spotify,

Nostalgify is a tiny Mac app that plays your Spotify through classic Winamp 2 skins. They're the real .wsz files people made in Winamp's heyday, preserved by the Internet Archive. Drop one in a folder and it just works.

⌨ Install it View on GitHub

Needs the Spotify desktop app. Apple Silicon and Intel.

nostalgify_demo.exe

↑ That's the real thing, running Webamp. Press play. The demo song is an original chiptune. The app plays your Spotify instead.

// FEATURES.TXT

Real skins, not lookalikes

Any classic Winamp 2 skin works. There are over 100,000 in the Internet Archive and the Winamp Skin Museum. Drop a .wsz into the skins folder and it shows up in the menu instantly. Cmd+R picks one at random.

Spotify does the work, out of sight

Nostalgify opens Spotify in the background and keeps it hidden. Play, pause, skip, seek, volume, shuffle and repeat all go straight to Spotify.

Scales without blurring

Drag the corner to make it bigger. Skins are pixel art, so they scale in crisp steps. Cmd+1, 2 and 3 give you single, double and triple size.

Muscle memory included

Z X C V B for previous, play, pause, stop and next. Arrow keys seek. Eject opens Spotify so you can pick something, then hops back.

A shelf for your music

Drag playlists, albums and artists from Spotify into Winamp's playlist window, or paste their links. Double-click to play. Liked Songs is always there.

No account, no login

Nostalgify talks to the Spotify app through macOS's built-in scripting. Nothing to sign up for, nothing sent anywhere.

// HOW_IT_WORKS.TXT

  1. Webamp draws the player. The open-source Webamp re-implements Winamp 2 in the browser, down to the skin format. Nostalgify runs it in a small window.
  2. Spotify plays the music. Nostalgify swaps Webamp's audio engine for one that forwards every button to Spotify through AppleScript.
  3. The display follows along. Once a second, Nostalgify asks Spotify what's playing and updates the marquee, clock and seek bar.

// INSTALL.EXE

Build it yourself in four commands. You'll need Git and Node.js 20 or newer.

Terminal 
git clone https://github.com/0xchaosbi/nostalgify.git
cd nostalgify
npm install
npm run install-app
  1. 1

    It builds and opens

    Nostalgify is built on your Mac, copied into Applications and opened. No "unidentified developer" warning, because you made it.

  2. 2

    Let it talk to Spotify

    macOS asks whether Nostalgify may control Spotify. Click OK.

  3. 3

    Press play

    Your first three skins download automatically. To update later, run git pull and the last two commands again.

Prefer a ready-made download?

Grab the latest .zip from Releases: arm64 for Apple Silicon, x64 for Intel. Unzip it and drag Nostalgify into Applications. Downloaded copies aren't signed by Apple, so the first time, right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. On newer macOS versions, go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

// GET_MORE_SKINS.TXT

Nostalgify comes with three skins. There are over 100,000 more in the Winamp Skin Museum, all free to browse.

  1. 1

    Find one

    Go to skins.webamp.org. Scroll, type in the Search... box, or hit Random Skin.

  2. 2

    Try it on

    Click a skin. It opens in a working player, so you can see it before you download it.

  3. 3

    Download

    Click Download under the player. You get a .wsz file.

  4. 4

    Drop it in

    In Nostalgify, choose Skins → Open Skins Folder and drag the file in. It shows up in the Skins menu straight away.

  • Prefer the Internet Archive? On a skin's page, find Download Options, click Show All, then click the file ending in .wsz.
  • Classic Winamp 2 skins only. Winamp 3 and 5 skins, which end in .wal, won't work.
  • If your browser saves a skin as .zip, drop it in as is. Don't unzip it.
  • Skins belong to their authors. The Readme button in the Skin Museum shows their notes.

// FAQ.TXT

Is this Winamp?

No. It's an independent fan project and isn't affiliated with Winamp or Spotify. It uses Webamp, an open-source re-implementation of the Winamp 2 interface, to display classic skins.

Does it work with a free Spotify account?

It should. Nostalgify only presses Spotify's own buttons, so it can do whatever your Spotify app allows on your plan.

Can I browse my whole Spotify library?

Not directly. Spotify only shares your library through its developer API, which would need every user to register as a Spotify developer. Instead, Nostalgify has a shelf: drag the playlists, albums and artists you love into the playlist window and they stay there. For anything else, press Eject, pick it in Spotify, and Nostalgify hops back.

Why is Spotify flashing when Nostalgify starts?

Spotify insists on coming to the front when it launches. Nostalgify hides it again straight away. Leaving Spotify open avoids the flash.

Nostalgify is free.

None of this exists without the people who saved these skins. If Nostalgify made you smile, please consider a donation to the Internet Archive. They keep the Winamp Skin Collection, and millions of other pieces of internet history, online for everyone.
